# Prompt: pre-mortem and challenge

Give this to a separate session or sub-agent that didn't write the strategy, like the fact-check.

---

It's 18 months from now and the **[TECHNOLOGY]** adoption described in `notes/strategy-brief.md` has failed. Work out
why, as a sceptical principal architect and a sceptical risk officer would.

1. **Failure stories.** Five distinct, plausible ways it failed: adoption, risk incident, cost, vendor change,
   operating model. Be specific about the chain of events.
2. **Weak assumptions.** Every assumption in the brief that one of those stories depends on, and how we'd test it now.
3. **Leading indicators.** For each failure, the earliest signal we'd see, and where we'd see it.
4. **Missing pieces.** Standards, controls, roles or measures the brief doesn't cover.
5. **Changes.** The smallest changes to the strategy that would prevent the most likely failures.

Report only; don't edit the brief. Under 900 words.
