# Prompt: strategy brief

Use once the pathway, threat model, cost model, current state and tests are in `notes/`.

---

Write the adoption strategy for **[TECHNOLOGY]** for **[AUDIENCE, e.g. architecture review board and CISO]**.

1. **One-page summary** first: the decision we're asking for, the outcome, the first wave, the cost, the top three
   risks and how they're controlled.
2. **Target state**: what's enabled, for whom, under which standards, by when. Use the waves and gates from the
   sequencer.
3. **Principles**: five to seven that settle future questions without a meeting.
4. **Operating model**: roles and a RACI for intake, approval, operation, review and retirement; who owns the
   platform; how makers get help.
5. **Roadmap**: waves with gates, dependencies on shared layers, and what's deliberately later.
6. **Measures**: adoption, value, risk and cost measures, each with a baseline, a target and a source of data.
7. **Risks and exit**: top risks with owners; what we'd do if the vendor changes terms, prices or features; how we'd
   leave.
8. **Decisions needed**, with dates.

Rules: every product fact cited; every estimate labelled; nothing in it that the notes don't support. Output
`notes/strategy-brief.md`, then list the claims a reviewer should challenge first.
