# Prompt: stakeholder briefs and training

---

Turn the pathway for **[TECHNOLOGY]** into material for each audience: **[e.g. executives, risk and compliance,
makers and developers, service desk]**.

1. For each audience: the three questions they'll ask, the answer in plain language, what they need to do
   differently, and where to go for help. No jargon they don't use.
2. A one-page brief per audience, plus an FAQ of ten questions with answers drawn only from the pathway and notes.
3. A training plan: what each audience learns, in what order, which site pages and practice levels they use, and
   how we'll know it worked.
4. Glossary terms any audience would trip on, ready to add to the site's glossary.

Output: `notes/briefs/`. Flag any answer that needs a decision we haven't made.
